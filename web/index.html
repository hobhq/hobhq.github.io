Autonomous household protocol

AI reasons. Code enforces. Hob reacts.

Who enforces an AI agent's rules when nobody is watching? Here, the household does. Pip, an AI shopper, spends from a shared vault on Base and Unichain and must say why. Alice and Bob agree on the rules together. Hob, a guardian on Reactive Network, watches both chains and enforces those rules by itself, even while Pip and the adults are offline. No bot, no cron, no admin.

      On-chain reactivity, in one household

      Seven questions, answered with transactions

      1. Can a contract react without a bot or keeper?

        Yes. The month resets with no transaction from anyone: the period id travels inside every spend event, and Hob keeps one ledger per period.

        Rollovers, below

      2. Observe, remember, evaluate, react?

        Hob subscribes to 3 events on 2 chains, keeps a ledger per period and category, compares it with the rules, and calls back every chain only when something must change.

        Every loop, with its transactions

      3. One shared view across chains?

        Alice spends on Base, Bob on Unichain. Neither vault can see the other. Hob sums both into one house budget and splits what is left per chain at the tighten line.

        The house ledger

      4. Stateful, not stateless?

        Pip's pace is counted across chains and over time: 6 buys an hour pass, the 7th freezes Pip everywhere. That needs memory of what already happened.

        Scenario: the pace limit is exact

      5. Who enforces an AI's rules when nobody is watching?

        The adults ratify the rules, the vaults enforce them on every purchase, and Hob enforces what no single chain can see. Pip can propose, never approve, co-sign or unfreeze.

        Refusals proven on-chain

      6. Can you audit what the agent did?

        Every purchase by Pip carries its reason on-chain, in the same transaction as the money. A purchase without one is refused. Hob's reactions are on Reactive, with their callbacks.

        Pip's receipts

      7. What happens when the agent goes offline?

        Nothing stops. Pip can sleep and the adults can be away: Hob keeps tightening, freezing, copying decisions and starting new periods.

        No transaction sent by anyone
      The case for Reactive

      Why this needs Reactive

      Without Hob

      • Each vault sees only its own chain.
      • Pip's 3 buys on Base and 4 on Unichain each look fine. Nobody notices 7.
      • Groceries can be overspent by shopping on both chains.
      • A rule changed on Base has to be re-entered on Unichain by hand, by someone with admin rights there.
      • The month resets only if a keeper or cron job remembers.
      • When Pip's session ends or the bot operator goes offline, nobody is watching. What the agent did lives in its operator's private logs.

      With Hob on Reactive Network

      • One household ledger, summed across chains.
      • Pip's pace counted across chains, then frozen on every chain.
      • Tight at the line (what's left is split per chain), frozen at the cap.
      • Rules and members are copied by callbacks only. There is no admin on the mirrors.
      • No cron: the period id travels inside every spend event.
      • Pip can sleep; Hob keeps watching. Pip's reasons and Hob's reactions are on-chain for anyone to check.

      Integration

      Reactive features used

      • Subscriptions to 3 event topics on 2 origin chains, registered after deploy (registerVault)
      • Stateful react(): a household ledger per period × category, plus role and velocity per member
      • Callbacks to every chain, including the chain the event came from
      • No cron: the period id is carried in the event
      • Idempotent: duplicate logs are skipped; stale or downgrading callbacks are ignored, not reverted (failed callbacks are still billed)
      • Sealed setup: after deploy, seal() removes every admin power. Nobody can add a vault or change a limit
      • Gas: the heaviest react() seen is ~493k (ledger + velocity freeze + 2 callbacks), under the 900k cap
      Craft

      Guarantees, tested

      • 62 tests: 55 unit tests (one per house rule, including "Pip must say why") and 7 fuzzed invariants (256 runs × 60 calls)
      • Never above the cap when Reactive answers before the next action
      • Overshoot under latency is no more than what is in flight; once delivered, every chain is FROZEN and the ledger equals the sum of the chains
      • Mutation testing: 18 planted bugs, all 18 caught
      • Local and synchronous checks on each vault: max per purchase, co-sign, per-role rate limit
